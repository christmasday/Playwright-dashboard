Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, member seats, and test volume.

What counts as a "recorded test execution"?
A recorded test is a single test('name', ...) block processed by our reporter in CI. If a build runs 50 tests, that consumes 50 test credits. Retries count toward the total if uploaded. Running tests locally without pointing to your dashboard API endpoint never consumes credits.
How do the team member / seat limits work?
The **Community tier** is dedicated to solo engineers (1 user account, no collaborator invites). The **Team tier** supports up to **3 team members**, making it ideal for dev pairs and small squads. The **Business tier** and **Enterprise tier** unlock **unlimited team seats**, ensuring your entire engineering department can triage traces without extra per-seat invoices.
What happens if my workspace exceeds monthly test quotas?
We believe in developer-first continuity. We never hard-block your CI pipelines. When a Free workspace hits 3,000 tests, we continue ingesting pass/fail status but temporarily pause heavy trace/video storage until the next cycle or upgrade. Paid plans can opt for automatic scalable overages ($3–$4 per 2,500 tests) so work never stops.
Can I still self-host Playwright Dashboard for free?
Yes! Our open-source Community distribution is 100% free and runnable on your own infrastructure via Docker and Docker Compose. Self-hosting means all traces and database records stay entirely within your private network perimeter.
Do you offer compliance packages and signed DPAs?
Yes. All cloud plans adhere strictly to EU/UK GDPR, CCPA, and Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023). For organizations requiring custom countersigned Data Processing Addendums (DPAs), vendor questionnaires, and SOC 2 Type II audit packages, these are provided under our Enterprise contracts.

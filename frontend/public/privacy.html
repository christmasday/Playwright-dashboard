Privacy Commitment

We do not sell test data, pipeline logs, or user information to data brokers or advertising networks.

Summary for Engineers: Playwright Dashboard ingests test results, duration metrics, flaky test patterns, screenshots, and trace artifacts generated during automated testing. We encrypt all data in transit and at rest, enforce strict role-based access, and provide automated pruning tools to keep your data footprint minimal.

1. Introduction & Roles

Playwright Dashboard (referred to as "Playwright Dashboard", "we", "us", or "our") provides an automated end-to-end (E2E) test visualization, flaky test detection, and test analytics platform accessible via our web application (playwright-dashboard.easytesting.app), API endpoints, CLI packages, and self-hosted distributions.

Depending on your relationship with us, we operate in two distinct capacities under data privacy regulations such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Data Controller: For account registration details, billing identifiers, marketing communications, and website usage telemetry of our direct customers.
  • Data Processor / Service Provider: For test execution artifacts, DOM snapshots, network payloads, console logs, and test metrics transmitted to our ingest API from your automated test runners or CI/CD pipelines. You (the customer) remain the Data Controller of this content.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three categories when you use our platform:

A. Account & Identity Data (Provided Directly)

  • Full name, email address, username, password hash (salted using bcrypt or Argon2).
  • Organization name, workspace preferences, and team member email invitations.
  • API keys, webhook URLs (e.g., Slack or Discord endpoints), and authentication tokens.

B. Test Execution & Ingestion Data (From CI/CD and Local Test Runs)

When you run the Playwright Dashboard reporter or send payloads to /api/tests/ingest, our system receives:

  • Test Metadata: Test suite names, file paths, test case titles, line numbers, execution tags, and retry counts.
  • Execution Statuses & Metrics: Passed, failed, timed out, skipped, duration (milliseconds), and worker process IDs.
  • Flaky Test Engine Telemetry: Historical pass/fail ratios across branches to calculate flakiness coefficients.
  • Test Artifacts: Failure screenshots (PNG/JPEG), browser console stdout/stderr logs, network request har files, Playwright trace zip files (trace.zip), and video captures.
  • CI/CD Environment Context: Git commit hash, branch name, author email (if configured in git), pull request number, CI provider name (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, Docker), and runner OS/browser versions.
Important Recommendation regarding Sensitive Test Data: Automated E2E tests should never record production passwords, payment card numbers, or real patient data. We strongly recommend configuring Playwright environment variables and redaction fixtures to sanitize secrets before uploading artifacts.

C. Technical & Operational Telemetry

  • IP addresses, browser user agent, referring URL, session timestamps, and device parameters.
  • System crash diagnostics and API response latency measurements.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are an individual located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, our legal bases under GDPR Article 6 include:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): To provide the services described in our Terms of Use, including ingesting and rendering your test reports.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To maintain platform security, prevent fraudulent abuse, and improve flaky test clustering algorithms without overriding your fundamental rights.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): To comply with tax, financial, and regulatory reporting mandates.

5. Data Retention & Automated Pruning

We believe in data minimization. You can configure data retention schedules directly in Workspace Settings:

  • Aggregated Test Summaries: Stored for the lifetime of your active workspace or up to 365 days on enterprise tiers.
  • Heavy Test Artifacts (Traces & Videos): Automatically pruned after 14 to 30 days (customizable) to conserve storage and reduce privacy liability.
  • Account Records: Retained as long as your workspace account remains open, and wiped within 30 days of verified account deletion.

7. International Data Transfers

When data is transferred from the EEA or UK to servers located in the United States, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and UK International Data Transfer Addendum, alongside robust technical safeguards including end-to-end encryption.

8. Security Safeguards

We implement defense-in-depth technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP traffic and WebSocket channels are enforced over TLS 1.3 with modern cipher suites and HSTS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database tables, backups, and artifact storage buckets are encrypted using AES-256 keys.
  • API Key Security: Project ingestion keys are hashed using SHA-256 before storage; raw tokens are never logged or exposed in client bundles.
  • Workspace Multi-Tenancy: Strict row-level security (RLS) policies prevent data leakage across distinct organization accounts.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request a machine-readable copy (JSON/CSV) of your personal account data and test history.
  • Right to Rectification: Update inaccurate or incomplete account credentials directly via Workspace Settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your account, projects, test runs, and traces.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Non-Discrimination: CCPA consumers will never be denied services or charged differentiated pricing for exercising statutory rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@playwright-dashboard.easytesting.app. We fulfill requests within 30 days free of charge.

10. Self-Hosted & On-Premise Deployments

If you run Playwright Dashboard on your own private infrastructure using Docker, Kubernetes, or self-hosted servers, zero test execution data or artifacts leave your perimeter. You hold sole controller and processor responsibilities for all data generated within self-hosted instances.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed strictly for professional software engineering and quality assurance workflows. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 16 years of age.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect emerging product features or regulatory changes. Material changes will be communicated via email notifications to workspace owners and prominent dashboard banners at least 14 days prior to effective adoption.

TL;DR: We only store what is strictly necessary to keep you authenticated and remember your UI dashboard settings (like theme and active project filter). We never install cross-site advertising cookies or sell browsing history.

1. What are Cookies & Browser Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when visiting websites. In addition to cookies, modern web applications utilize localStorage and sessionStorage to preserve client-side state across page reloads without burdening every network request.

2. Categories of Storage We Use

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies and storage tokens are indispensable for you to log in, navigate between projects, and safely communicate with our WebSocket real-time cluster. Without these, the application cannot function.

B. Functional & User Preference Storage

These keys remember choices you make in the dashboard UI, such as dark mode preference, collapsed state of navigation drawers, selected test filters, and build comparison metrics.

C. Operational & Performance Telemetry

Aggregated diagnostics used to monitor frontend script crashes and API latency to maintain platform stability.

3. Inventory of Keys & Cookies

Identifier Type Duration Purpose
token / sb-auth-token Cookie / LocalStorage Session (up to 7 days) Encrypted JWT session authentication token for workspace access.
theme LocalStorage Persistent Stores UI color scheme preference (Dark / Light mode).
sidebar_collapsed LocalStorage Persistent Remembers navigation sidebar expansion state.
active_project_id SessionStorage Browser Session Retains currently selected test project context during navigation.
flaky_filter_threshold LocalStorage Persistent Remembers custom flakiness score filter settings.

4. Zero Advertising Trackers Guarantee

Playwright Dashboard is a developer productivity platform. We do not embed third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads), or data-broker trackers. Your browsing habits within the test dashboard are strictly private to your workspace.

5. Managing & Clearing Cookies in Your Browser

You can configure your browser to notify you when cookies are set or refuse all cookies. If you block strictly necessary cookies or local storage, you will be unable to log in to the dashboard:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

6. Updates to this Policy

We may update this policy if new features require client-side persistence mechanisms. Revisions will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

7. Questions

If you have questions about how we use cookies, email us at privacy@playwright-dashboard.easytesting.app.

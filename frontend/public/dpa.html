Enterprise Note: This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) automatically supplements the Terms of Use for any customer processing Personal Data originating from the European Union, UK, or Switzerland through Playwright Dashboard.

1. Preamble & Definitions

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is entered into between Playwright Dashboard ("Processor") and the entity subscribing to or utilizing the service ("Customer" or "Controller").

  • "Applicable Data Protection Law" means GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), UK GDPR, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and CCPA/CPRA.
  • "Personal Data", "Data Subject", "Processing", and "Personal Data Breach" shall have the meanings ascribed in GDPR Article 4.
  • "Standard Contractual Clauses" (SCCs) means the standard contractual clauses approved by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.

2. Scope & Processing Roles

The parties acknowledge that with respect to Customer Data ingested during automated Playwright test runs, Customer is the Data Controller and Playwright Dashboard is the Data Processor acting strictly on Customer's documented instructions.

3. Processor Obligations

Playwright Dashboard warrants and commits to:

  • Process Customer Personal Data solely in accordance with Customer's documented instructions and the Terms of Use.
  • Ensure all staff authorized to process Customer Data have executed binding confidentiality commitments.
  • Implement and maintain the Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs) specified in Annex 2.

4. Sub-processors

Customer grants general written authorization to Playwright Dashboard to engage sub-processors to assist in platform hosting, database storage, and caching.

  • Current approved sub-processors are published in our Privacy Policy Sub-processors Registry.
  • We will notify Customer of any intended changes or additions to sub-processors at least 30 days in advance. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within 14 days of notice.

5. Security of Processing

Taking into account the state of the art and the risks of processing, Processor implements appropriate technical safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access control (RBAC), and automated continuous vulnerability scans.

6. Incident & Breach Notification (Within 72 Hours)

In the event of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Data, Playwright Dashboard will notify Customer without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach. The notification will describe the nature of the incident, affected data categories, and remediation steps taken.

7. Data Subject Rights Assistance

Processor shall, taking into account the nature of processing, provide reasonable technical assistance to enable Customer to fulfill its obligations to respond to Data Subject requests under GDPR Chapter III (such as access, rectification, portability, or deletion).

8. Return or Erasure of Personal Data

Upon termination of the underlying subscription agreement, Playwright Dashboard will delete all Customer Data and existing copies within thirty (30) days, unless applicable European Union or Member State law requires retention.

9. Audits & Compliance Verification

Processor will make available to Customer all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with GDPR Article 28, and allow for audits conducted by Customer or an independent auditor subject to reasonable confidentiality and advance notice.

10. International Transfers & Standard Contractual Clauses

Where transfer of Customer Data involves cross-border transmission outside the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, the parties agree that Module 2 (Controller-to-Processor) of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914) shall be incorporated by reference.

Annex 1: Details of Data Processing

Element Description
Subject Matter Ingestion, processing, aggregation, and visualization of automated Playwright test execution results.
Duration Term of the Customer workspace subscription plus 30-day graceful deletion window.
Nature & Purpose Rendering real-time dashboards, flaky test analytics, trace viewer artifacts, and CI/CD status reporting.
Data Categories Test execution logs, trace files, failure screenshots, git commit metadata (author names/emails), developer login credentials.
Data Subjects Customer software engineers, QA automation professionals, test operators, and end-users whose test mock data might appear in traces.

Annex 2: Technical & Organizational Security Measures (TOMs)

  • Access Control: Multi-factor authentication support, scoped API tokens, principle of least privilege.
  • Data Transmission: Enforced TLS 1.3, strict Content Security Policies, signed download URLs for test artifacts.
  • Data Storage: AES-256 envelope encryption for database records and object storage traces.
  • Business Continuity: Daily automated backups with geo-redundant storage and tested disaster recovery plans.
